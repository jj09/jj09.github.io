In Bill Perkins’ book ***[Die with Zero](https://diewithzerobook.com/)***, there is a fundamental premise that permanently rewired how I think about time and health:

> *"Money without health and time is useless. To maximize your life’s fulfillment, you must align your experiences with the specific phases of your healthspan - before your biological clock runs out."*

To plan your life, your career, your financial independence, and your bucket list, you need a realistic estimate of your **longevity runway**.

One way to do that is Dr. Thomas Perls’ *[Living to 100 Calculator](https://www.livingto100.com/calculator)*. It is rooted in clinical data from the **[New England Centenarian Study](https://www.bumc.bu.edu/centenarian/)**. However, it's a **static quiz**: you answer 40 questions, hit Submit, get a single fixed number, and leave.

It does not give you answer to the questions that actually matter:
1. *"What happens to my projected lifespan if I fix my sleep and drop my resting heart rate by 8 bpm?"*
2. *"Which single habit will give me the **most bang for my buck** to live longer with the least effort?"*

Between **Bryan Johnson’s** multi-million-dollar *Blueprint* protocol, **Dr. Peter Attia’s** *Outlive* and VO2 max research, and the timeless community wisdom of the **Blue Zones**, we are living in a golden age of longevity science. But most of us aren't going to take 100 supplements a day or spend \$2M/year on biohacking clinics. We need to know: **what actually moves the needle most efficiently?**

I ~~vibed~~ built **[Longevita](https://jj09.net/longevita/)** - an interactive lifespan simulator with real-time parameter tweaking and a built-in **"Bang for Your Buck" Longevity ROI engine**.

### What Makes Longevita Different?

**1. Zero-Latency "What-If" Sandbox**
Longevita lets you drag sliders and toggle habits in real-time across 29+ evidence-based biomarkers and behaviors:
* **Blood Pressure & Glycemic Control** (Systolic/Diastolic BP, Fasting Glucose, HbA1c, Lipids/ApoB)
* **Cardiorespiratory Conditioning** (Zone 2 cardio, progressive resistance training, daily steps, sedentary hours)
* **Blue Zone Nutrition** (Mediterranean dietary pattern, polyphenols/green tea, fiber diversity, minimal ultra-processed food)
* **Psychosocial & Sleep Biology** (7–8h restful sleep, chronic stress resilience, Blue Zone *Moai* social connection)

As you tweak any parameter, the central **Radial Gauge**, **Biological Age vs. Chronological Age**, and **Pillar Waterfall Chart** update with zero lag.

**2. The "Bang for Your Buck" Longevity ROI Index**

$$\text{Bang-for-the-Buck Index} = \left(\frac{\Delta \text{Years Gained}}{\text{Behavioral Effort Score (1–5)}}\right) \times 10$$
